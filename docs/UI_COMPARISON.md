The mock's login “Explore as …” second CTA, the autofill enable switch, the five-step upload checklist, and the extraction tab group are recorded as absent affordances rather than hidden or implied features. The global rail intentionally has more destinations than the mock because it includes the full product module set.

The per-row commands verify the relevant route and behavior. The complete browser gate is `TANTARA_WEB_URL=http://127.0.0.1:3010 TANTARA_API_ORIGIN=http://127.0.0.1:8010 pnpm exec playwright test --reporter=line`.
